# AISIKAI catalog

Marketing site for Jiangsu AISIKAI electrical products, sold in Indonesia through Deta Power.

## Language

**Product**:
A named electrical device a buyer can open, inquire about, or download a brochure for.
_Avoid_: item, SKU (unless talking to factory systems)

**Category**:
A buyer-facing family of Products (ATS, MCCB, MCB, and the rest). Identified by a slug in the URL.
_Avoid_: group, type, collection

**Catalog query**:
How a buyer asks for Products: by Product id, Category slug, or search text. The URL is the seam.
_Avoid_: filter, location state

**Inquiry**:
A request to talk to Deta Power about a Product (WhatsApp, email, or the contact form).
_Avoid_: lead, ticket
