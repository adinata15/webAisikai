# Home Page Overrides

> Rules in this file **override** `design-system/aisikai/MASTER.md`.
> Only deviations from the Master are documented here. For all other rules, refer to the Master.

## Deviations

### Hero claim

Keep the two banner photos. Add a readable panel over them: one sentence (high-quality electrical switches since 2007), two actions (View products, Contact), and a short proof row (ISO 9001, CCC, CE, SGS). Previous, next, pause, and slide dots sit together on the banner bottom-right, clear of the claim card. Reduced motion shows a static first slide.

### Proof strip

After the hero, and before Featured Products, a compact proof strip: year, certifications, and product lines.

### Featured tabs

Featured Products stays a real tablist with arrow-key support. It is the product overview in the section order, after the proof strip.

### Shortened about

The home About block is a wide click-to-play film (poster first; YouTube mounts on play), one paragraph, and the four value cards. The long history stays on `/about-us`.

### Grid closing band

The closing band is a two-column grid: copy, then the product image. It does not use negative margins or absolute offsets.
