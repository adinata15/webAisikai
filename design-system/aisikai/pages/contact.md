# Contact Page Overrides

> Rules in this file **override** `design-system/aisikai/MASTER.md`.
> Only deviations from the Master are documented here. For all other rules, refer to the Master.

## Deviations

### Form error summary

Keep the live status message. Add an error summary that lists invalid fields and moves focus to the first invalid field. Do not communicate errors by green or red color alone.

### Agency row

"Our Agency" is a horizontal row of partner agencies: flag, country name, and email (South Africa, Cambodia, Turkiye, Romania, Vietnam). It is a partner list. AISIKAI stays the brand; Deta Power is not the heading of this row.

### Map

A full-width factory map sits under the agency row, with an accessible name.
